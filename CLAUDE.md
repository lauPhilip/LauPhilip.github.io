# Design rules for this portfolio

These rules are set by Philip and apply to every change to the site: HTML, CSS, JS and the copy in `data/` and `projects/`. Anyone (human or agent) editing the site must check their work against this list before handing it back. A change that introduces any of these is not done.

## Not allowed

1. Arrows on every button (↗, →, chevrons as decoration). An arrow is only acceptable where it carries real meaning, such as previous/next in a carousel.
2. Little dots between words (`·`, `•` as separators). Use proper sentences, commas, line breaks or layout instead.
3. Overused eyebrows (small uppercase kicker labels above headings or cards).
4. Cream or warm off-white backgrounds. Use neutral white / neutral greys.
5. One italic word in a headline or header.
6. Sparkle icons (✦, ✨, the "AI sparkle").
7. The word "quiet".
8. Numbered sections (01, 02, 03 … next to headings or nav items).
9. Everything in a card. Cards are for genuinely card-like content (e.g. projects); text sections are laid out without boxes.
10. Pulsing green dots (status dots, "live" pulses).
11. Blurry glow behind the hero (radial gradients, blurred blobs).
12. Instrument Serif.
13. Coloured left borders (e.g. on quotes or callouts).
14. Three icon cards in a row.
15. Pills above headlines (status/badge pills placed over a heading).
16. The word "real".
17. Made-up stats. Every number on the site must be true and traceable to Philip's actual work.
18. Two-word sentences ("Ship fast." "Built different.").
19. Sections fading or sliding in when scrolling (no reveal-on-scroll animations).
20. Dithering (dither patterns, halftone or noise textures).

## How to check

Before finishing, grep the repo for the obvious offenders (`·`, `quiet`, `\breal\b`, `Instrument`, `border-left`, `pulse`, `radial-gradient`, `i-spark`, `i-arrow`, `reveal`) and look at the rendered page in light and dark mode, on desktop and mobile.

## Architecture

The site is static HTML generated by `build.py` (Python 3.12+, standard library only). Content lives in `data/*.json` and `projects/<id>/info.json`; `build.py` writes `index.html`, `work/index.html`, `work/<slug>/index.html`, `about/index.html`, `404.html`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `data/ask-index.json`. Never edit those generated files by hand: change the data or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, and commit the output. The GitHub Actions workflow runs `python build.py --check` and fails if the committed pages are out of date.

Styling is one file, `css/style.css` (tokens at the top, dark mode through `prefers-color-scheme`). `js/site.js` adds analytics, copy-email, Work filters and optional clips; `js/ask.js` runs the Ask panel from `data/ask-index.json` and the optional Worker in `ask-worker/`. Pages must stay fully readable with JavaScript turned off.
