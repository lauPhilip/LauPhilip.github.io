About

I work where technology meets the business. I start with the problem and what it costs, build the smallest thing that solves it, and stay until people use it.

Most of my work deals with knowledge that is hard to find, such as manuals, standards, course material and research papers. I turn it into tools that answer questions and show where each answer comes from. Next, I want to build AI and software full time, and over the years grow into shaping technology strategy.

Danish and English. Outside work: technology, computer games, Marvel, LEGO and Olympic weightlifting.
