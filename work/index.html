Work

Fourteen projects from industry, research and my own time. Each one says what I built and what came of it.

  • MIND: AI Search Assistant for LEGO Engineers

    An AI assistant used by 331 engineers at the LEGO Group to search their technical documentation. Each answer shows the document it comes from.

    Main developer, as a student worker at the LEGO Group (also my MSc thesis)

  • AskEIVA: AI Support Assistant for EIVA

    An AI assistant built to help EIVA's customer service team find answers. It searches the company's technical manuals and past support tickets.

    Aarhus University, developer, together with EIVA

  • Shrinkage Analysis Request Tool

    An internal app that 35–50 colleagues use every week to request and follow shrinkage analyses of moulded parts. I maintained and improved it.

    The LEGO Group, maintainer and developer

  • Automated Engineering Newsletter

    An automated weekly newsletter that gathers engineering news and sends it to 190+ colleagues. Each reader gets only the topics they signed up for.

    The LEGO Group

  • Quality and Release Process for Low-Code Apps

    A clear, step-by-step process for employees who build their own apps, from idea to release, with quality checks along the way.

    The LEGO Group, designed the process

  • Digitalization Strategy for Local Planning

    An internship business case at the LEGO Group. It looked at how to replace many separate Excel sheets in local planning with one shared planning tool.

    Engineering intern, the LEGO Group (Mould Engineering Prototypes)

  • Power Apps Christmas Calendar

    A digital Christmas calendar for colleagues, built in Power Apps. A new door opens each day in December with a quiz or a small game.

    Personal project

  • TraceableAI: Literature Reviews With Checked Citations

    Literature reviews written by AI, where every citation can be checked. For each cited sentence, it shows the passage in the paper that supports it, or says plainly that it found none.

    Aarhus University, designer and developer, first author of the paper

  • Coursebot: AI Course Assistants for Teachers

    A platform where university teachers set up an AI chatbot for their course, without writing code. Students ask it questions, and it answers only from the course material.

    Aarhus University, main developer (frontend, backend, design and testing)

  • Open Door: A Classroom Game for Threat Modelling

    A free browser game that teaches threat modelling. Student teams walk through a made-up company, spot what could go wrong, and then see if they saw the incident coming.

    Aarhus University, creator and developer

  • Generative AI Business Case

    A business case on whether and how a global design team should start using generative AI. It weighs costs, benefits and risks.

    Aarhus University, bachelor's thesis, written with a fellow student

  • LUMA: Personal AI Desktop Assistant

    My personal AI assistant for the desktop. Say "Luma" to wake it. Then you can chat, check the news and markets, use the camera, run commands or play music.

    Personal project, sole developer

  • LUMA Brain: A Language Model From Scratch

    The "brain" behind LUMA. It is a small AI language model that I am building step by step, so the assistant can one day run fully on my own computer.

    Personal learning project

  • Online Yoga Platform

    A home yoga platform I built for my girlfriend. It has a video library with routines and a log to track progress over time.

    Personal project
