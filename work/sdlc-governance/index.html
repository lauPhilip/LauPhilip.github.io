Quality and Release Process for Low-Code Apps

A clear, step-by-step process for employees who build their own apps, from idea to release, with quality checks along the way.

Where
The LEGO Group
My role
Designed the process
Status
Finished

The problem

Many teams built apps on the Power Platform without a shared way of working. That made quality and IT rules hard to manage.

What I built

I designed a model that covers requirements, separate test and live environments, testing and release approvals. Then I agreed it with the stakeholders.

The result

Teams could keep building quickly while meeting IT's rules.

How it works

The model defines each stage from requirements to release: separate development, test and production environments, test steps, and approval gates before release. I agreed the model with several business stakeholders and IT, balancing speed for the app makers against IT's release and compliance rules.

Technologies: Power Platform, Governance, Release management, Citizen development
