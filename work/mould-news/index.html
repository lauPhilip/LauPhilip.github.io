Automated Engineering Newsletter

An automated weekly newsletter that gathers engineering news and sends it to 190+ colleagues. Each reader gets only the topics they signed up for.

Where
The LEGO Group
Status
Finished

The problem

Engineering news had to reach many colleagues, but not everyone wanted every topic.

What I built

Colleagues submit news in a simple app, and readers pick their topics in another. Every week an automated flow sorts the news into three topic streams, builds a formatted email and sends it out.

The result

Sent every week to 190+ colleagues, each with their own topics.

How it works

Two Power Apps: one for submitting news and one where readers manage their topics. A scheduled Power Automate flow runs weekly. It sorts the news into three topic streams, builds an HTML email from nested tables and sends it only to current subscribers.

Technologies: Power Apps, Power Automate, HTML email
