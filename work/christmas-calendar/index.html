Power Apps Christmas Calendar

A digital Christmas calendar for colleagues, built in Power Apps. A new door opens each day in December with a quiz or a small game.

Where
Personal project
Status
Finished

The problem

I wanted to lift spirits and bring colleagues together in December.

What I built

Each of the 24 doors unlocks on its own date, and behind each one is a quiz question or a mini-game.

The result

A December calendar for colleagues with a new quiz or game each day.

For engineers: how it works

Each door checks the date on the user's device and stays locked until its day. There are 24 screens, each with its own quiz or mini-game, built in Power Apps.

Technologies: Power Apps, Gamification, UI design
