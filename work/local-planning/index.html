Digitalization Strategy for Local Planning

An internship business case at the LEGO Group. It looked at how to replace many separate Excel sheets in local planning with one shared planning tool.

Where
The LEGO Group
My role
Engineering intern, the LEGO Group (Mould Engineering Prototypes)
Status
Finished

The problem

Local planning was done in many different Excel sheets. Each planner worked in their own way, and much of the knowledge was in people's heads.

What I built

I interviewed 7 local planners and mapped how planning was done in practice. I wrote a 60-page business proposal with the economics, the business case, user requirements and recommendations. I also built a rapid prototype in Power Apps, wrote a one-page brief for leadership and presented it to management.

The result

The LEGO Group later approved Kinaxis Maestro as a unified planning tool, a need my analysis helped document.

How it works

Method: interviews with 7 local planners, a map of the current planning process across their Excel sheets, a rapid Power Apps prototype to test a shared way of working, and a 60-page business proposal covering economics, business case, user requirements and recommendations. The findings were condensed into a one-page brief and presented to management.

Technologies: Digitalization strategy, Business case, Requirements, Power Apps, Prototyping
