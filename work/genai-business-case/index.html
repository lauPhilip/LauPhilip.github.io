Generative AI Business Case

A business case on whether and how a global design team should start using generative AI. It weighs costs, benefits and risks.

Where
Aarhus University
My role
Bachelor's thesis, written with Michael Wulff
Status
Finished

The problem

The LEGO Group's Graphic Design Team wanted to know if generative AI could help its designers, and at what cost.

What I built

We studied how generative AI could support the designers and compared different ways to introduce it. We weighed the time it could save against the cost and effort.

The result

The report gave clear recommendations on legal risks, challenges in the organisation and the financial impact. The team's leadership wrote us a letter of recommendation.

How it works

We compared several implementation scenarios. For each one we weighed time saved for designers against cost and effort. The recommendations covered legal risk, challenges in the organisation and the financial impact.

Technologies: Business case, Generative AI, Feasibility study, Financial modelling
